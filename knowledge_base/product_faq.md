# NorthPeak Goods — Product FAQ

## What we sell
- NorthPeak Goods sells home and kitchen appliances (blenders, coffee makers, air fryers, kettles), footwear, and outdoor gear (backpacks, tents, water bottles).

## Damaged or defective items
- If an item arrives damaged or broken, or stops working within 30 days of delivery, the customer can choose a free replacement or a full refund, including original shipping charges.
- To request a replacement:
  1. Open "My Orders" and select the order.
  2. Click "Report a problem" and choose "Damaged or defective".
  3. Upload one or two photos showing the damage or fault (a short video is accepted for appliances that do not power on).
  4. A replacement is dispatched within 2 business days once the report is approved, which normally takes 1 business day.
- The customer does not need to send the damaged item back unless the support team asks for it; if a return is needed, a free prepaid label is provided.

## Warranty
- All appliances (including blenders, coffee makers and air fryers) carry a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date covering manufacturing defects.
- After the first 30 days and within the warranty period, defective appliances are repaired or replaced at no cost. Warranty claims are submitted through "My Orders > Report a problem".
- The warranty does not cover damage from drops, misuse, or use with incorrect voltage.

## Blender care and safety
- Never run the blender empty for more than 10 seconds.
- If the motor stops suddenly, the overheating protection has been triggered: unplug the blender and wait 20 minutes before using it again.
- The jar, lid and blade assembly are dishwasher safe on the top rack. The motor base must only be wiped with a damp cloth.

## Footwear sizing
- Our shoes follow standard US sizing. If you are between sizes, we recommend ordering the larger size.
- Size exchanges are free within 30 days of delivery (see the Returns and Refund Policy).

## Product availability
- Out-of-stock items show a "Notify me" button. Customers who click it receive an email when the item is back in stock.
- We do not take pre-orders or hold stock for customers.
