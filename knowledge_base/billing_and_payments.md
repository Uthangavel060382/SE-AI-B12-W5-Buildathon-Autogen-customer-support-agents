# NorthPeak Goods — Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- We do not accept cash on delivery, cheques or bank transfers.

## When the card is charged
- The card is authorised when the order is placed and charged when the order is dispatched.
- If an order is split into several shipments, the customer may see one charge per shipment. The total of all charges equals the order total.

## Seeing more than one charge
- A temporary authorisation ("pending" charge) can appear alongside the final charge. Pending authorisations disappear automatically within 3–5 business days.
- If the same amount has been charged (not just pending) more than once for a single order, this is a duplicate charge. Duplicate charges are handled by the Billing team, who will investigate and refund confirmed duplicates within 5 business days.

## Payment disputes and chargebacks
- Payment disputes, chargebacks, suspected fraud and unauthorised charges are always handled by a human member of the Billing team, never by automated replies.
- Customers can contact billing@northpeakgoods.com or wait for a Billing specialist to reply to their ticket within 1 business day.

## Promo codes
- Only one promo code can be used per order. Promo codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed.
- Price adjustments are offered if an item's price drops within 7 days of delivery; contact support with the order ID.

## Invoices
- A PDF invoice for every order can be downloaded from "My Orders > View invoice".
