# NorthPeak Goods — Returns and Refund Policy

## Return window
- Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund.
- Items must be unused, in their original condition, and in the original packaging with tags attached.
- Footwear must be returned in the original shoe box, and must not show signs of outdoor wear.
- Final-sale items, opened personal-care products and custom-engraved items cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
1. Sign in to your account and open "My Orders".
2. Select the order and click "Return an item".
3. Choose the item(s) and a reason for the return.
4. Print the prepaid return label that is emailed to you (no printer? choose "QR code" and show it at any partner drop-off point).
5. Drop the package off within 14 days of creating the return.

## Return shipping cost
- Returns are free for defective, damaged or incorrect items.
- For all other returns (for example wrong size or change of mind), a $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection.
- Customers receive an email when the refund is issued. Banks may take an additional 3–5 business days to show the refund on a statement.
- Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was defective, damaged or incorrect.

## Exchanges
- Exchanges for a different size or colour of the same item are free and can be requested from "My Orders" within 30 days of delivery, subject to stock.

## Items received damaged or defective
- See the Product FAQ section "Damaged or defective items" for the replacement process. Customers may choose either a free replacement or a full refund.
