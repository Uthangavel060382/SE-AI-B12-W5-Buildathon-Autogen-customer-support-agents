# NorthPeak Goods — Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines
- Standard Shipping: 3–5 business days after the order is dispatched. Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99.
- Express Shipping: 1–2 business days after dispatch. Costs $14.99.
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM (Eastern Time) on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and public holidays, are dispatched the next business day.
- We currently ship only within the United States and Canada. Canadian orders take 5–8 business days.

## Tracking your order
- A shipping confirmation email with a tracking number is sent as soon as the order is dispatched.
- Customers can also track an order at northpeakgoods.com/track by entering the order ID (format ORD-1234) and the email address used at checkout.
- Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after the confirmation email is sent.

## Late or delayed orders
- Carrier delays of 1–2 business days beyond the estimated delivery date are common during peak seasons and bad weather.
- If an order is more than 2 business days past its estimated delivery date, the customer should contact support with the order ID. We will open a trace request with the carrier within 1 business day.
- If the carrier cannot locate the package within 5 business days of the trace request, we will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.
- If Express Shipping arrives late, the express shipping fee ($14.99) is refunded automatically.

## Package shows delivered but not received
- Check around the delivery location, with neighbours, and with the building's mail room.
- Wait 24 hours, as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early.
- If the package is still missing after 24 hours, contact support with the order ID and we will open a missing-package claim.

## Changing the shipping address
- The address can be changed free of charge until the order is dispatched, from the "My Orders" page.
- After dispatch, the address cannot be changed by NorthPeak; the customer may request a redirect through the carrier's own website using the tracking number.
