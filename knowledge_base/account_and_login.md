# NorthPeak Goods — Account and Login Help

## Resetting a forgotten password
1. Go to northpeakgoods.com/login and click "Forgot password?".
2. Enter the email address linked to your account.
3. Open the reset email (it arrives within 5 minutes — check the spam or promotions folder).
4. Click the reset link. The link is valid for 60 minutes and can be used only once.
5. Choose a new password with at least 10 characters, including one number and one symbol.

## Account locked after too many attempts
- After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes for security.
- After 30 minutes, the customer can try again or reset the password using the steps above.

## Not receiving the reset email
- Check the spam, junk and promotions folders.
- Make sure the email address is the one used to create the account (customers sometimes check out as a guest, in which case no account exists).
- Add support@northpeakgoods.com to the address book and request the email again.
- If the email still does not arrive after 30 minutes, contact support and an agent will verify identity and send a manual reset link.

## Two-step verification
- Two-step verification can be turned on in "Account Settings > Security".
- If a customer has lost access to their verification phone, they must contact support; an agent will verify identity using the order history before removing two-step verification.

## Changing the email address on the account
- Sign in, open "Account Settings > Profile", and update the email address. A confirmation link is sent to the new address and must be clicked within 24 hours.

## Guest orders
- Orders placed as a guest can be tracked at northpeakgoods.com/track using the order ID and email. A guest order can be linked to a new account created with the same email address.
